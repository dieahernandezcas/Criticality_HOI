Preprint · 2025

Higher-Order Statistical Structure Emerges from
Nonlinear Dynamics Without Explicit Higher-Order Coupling

A minimal three-node Wilson–Cowan network study comparing four coupling architectures and quantifying higher-order statistical dependencies.

Status: In Development License: MIT Python 3.9+
View on GitHub

Abstract

Higher-order statistical dependencies are increasingly reported in complex dynamical systems and neural population activity, where they are often interpreted as signatures of explicit higher-order interactions. Whether such dependencies require higher-order coupling in the underlying equations of motion, or can emerge generically from nonlinear second-order dynamics, remains unclear. We address this question using a minimal three-node Wilson–Cowan network with all-to-all connectivity, comparing four coupling architectures: second-order additive, second-order diffusive, explicit third-order additive, and explicit third-order diffusive. We vary external drive P, coupling strength K, and sigmoid slope aE, and quantify higher-order statistical structure using total correlation minus dual total correlation (TC − DTC) and third-order cumulants (κ123).
Main finding: In the second-order additive model, TC − DTC in the oscillatory regime is on average 16-fold above the fixed-point baseline, reaching values comparable to the explicit third-order model. Increasing aE amplifies TC − DTC up to 28-fold above baseline — independently of coupling order. Proximity to a Hopf bifurcation and local nonlinear gain are stronger determinants than coupling architecture.

Coupling Architectures

The four excitatory coupling architectures implemented in this study differ in the structural order of the inter-node interaction and whether coupling is additive or diffusive.

PA — Second-Order Additive
𝒞i = K Σj Mij Ej

Standard second-order coupling. Higher-order structure arises solely from the nonlinear sigmoid.

PD — Second-Order Diffusive
𝒞i = K Σj Mij(Ej−Ei)

Diffusive coupling vanishes for homogeneous states; suppresses common-mode activity.

TA — Third-Order Additive
𝒞i = K3 Σjk Tijk EjEk

Explicit higher-order coupling; the interaction is quadratic before the sigmoid.

TD — Third-Order Diffusive
𝒞i = K3 Σjk Tijk(Ej−Ei)(Ek−Ei)

Diffusive higher-order coupling; vanishes for synchronous states.

Solid edges = additive coupling  |  Dashed edges = diffusive coupling  |  Shaded triangle = explicit 3rd-order hyperedge
